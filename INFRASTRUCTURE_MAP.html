Three decisions that must not be quietly reversed

Discount stacks

An extra discount applies on top of a chosen volume column, with a visible warning whenever it does. Tier prices already run ~50% under list — stacking silently would give margin away.

Missing prices fall back

A product with no price in the chosen column keeps its list price rather than disappearing from the customer's document. The panel reports how many did.

Duplicates are reported

Duplicate part numbers are never deduped. Dropping one silently would mean picking a price at random and hiding a commercial problem.

The July 7 build, and why it matters

The price list tab existed for six weeks in no commit at all — it survived only inside a build sitting in OneDrive\WEB offert\files\, minutes from being deleted as a stale duplicate. It is committed and live now, but that 3,170,524-byte build is still the only artefact of a lineage the repo never captured.

Running it locally

file:// is blocked by both the preview tool and Playwright, so serve it over localhost. npx serve fails on the space in C:\Program Files — use Python's own server.

python -m http.server 8142 --directory .

note The repo's .claude/launch.json defines one server, cc-offert on 8142. The handoff also mentions 8140 and 8143 — those are not in the file. .claude/ is gitignored, so this config is machine-local and not backed up anywhere.

The only real backup is the JSON export

The quote archive can be exported and re-imported wholesale as JSON. That is the sole route by which a quote survives a cleared browser profile or moves to another machine — and it covers quotes only. Saved price lists have no export and no loader.

F

The catalogue baked into the file

Read at startup from a <script type="application/json"> block, and replaceable for a session by uploading a workbook. Source: TG_kalkyl_och_valuta_updated.xlsx.

16 families · 86 products

Plus 43 accessories in two sections — Accessories (7) and Cables (36). They are stored in a different shape: {part_number, description, price_eur} with no tiers and no list price. plSections() is the single adapter that normalises both, so the picker, pricing engine, preview, PDF and Excel never need to know accessories exist. Add accessory handling there, not in five places.

Manufacturing cost coverage

1 of 86 products carries an mk_sek value. Margin is fully built and has almost nothing to calculate from.

The importer is already prepared: it matches a cost column on the start of the normalised header, because real headers carry qualifiers (Cost (CMBF ONLY), MK (SEK)), and excludes look-alikes like Cost center. Currency comes from the header — EUR if it says so, otherwise SEK. The day the column appears it is read with no code change.

The margin model, mirroring TG_kalkyl_och_valuta.xlsx

Sheet B12 — gross margin

TG = (SP − MK) / SP

Sheet B13 — gross profit

TB = SP − MK

Sheet B14 — price to hit target

SP* = MK / (1 − target)

for information The source workbook's B12 reads 1 - ABS((E5-B6)/B5) and references E5, which is empty. It gives the right answer only because empty evaluates to zero. If anyone types into E5, the sheet silently starts computing something else.

G

Rules and traps

Standing constraints, and the specific mistakes that have cost time on this codebase. Filter by kind.

Customer-facing · hard rule

Cost and margin never reach the customer

The Margin (TG) card is internal and badged “never printed”. Nothing about cost may appear in plRenderDoc(), either PDF, or the Excel export. Verify by decompressing the PDF streams and scanning the xlsx XML — not by reading the screen.

Customer-facing · hard rule

The document never names its basis column

Which volume column a price list was calculated from is panel-only feedback. It does not go into plRenderDoc.

Customer-facing · hard rule

One rate for both tabs

A single sekPerEur() reads the Currency card. Never go back to meta.tg_assumptions.fx_sek_eur — it is a snapshot frozen at import, not a rate. Using it made the same product read 66.2% on the quote and 68.7% on the price list.

Customer-facing · hard rule

The forecast condition lives in six places

Added 2026-08-21. The sentence renders from QUOTE_FORECAST_TEXT on the quote’s two surfaces and PL_FORECAST_TEXT on the price list’s three, with a fourth hand-kept copy inside generate_pdf.py. The wording is a fixed constant, not a free-text field \u2014 it is a commercial condition, and a typo in a customer document costs more than the flexibility is worth. buildExportObj() carries forecast_condition; a missing key reads as false, so quotes archived before the change still render.

CSS & layout

Styling is duplicated three times

The app's <style>, the pdfmake style objects, and generate_pdf.py's f-string CSS. A change to the customer document must be made in all three or the PDFs silently diverge from the screen.

CSS & layout

Tier A is an appended override block

217 lines fenced with a banner comment, sitting before @media print so print still wins. Deleting the block reverts the look exactly. To consolidate, fold the overrides into the originals and delete the block — never keep both.

CSS & layout

A hover state is a promise

Archive rows were given an orange hover while only the small Open button was wired, which read as “the archive won't open”. Wire the click or do not add the hover.

Verification

Audit the UI in a non-default state

Controls that have not rendered yet audit as absent, and the audit then reports success. A claim of “77 of 77 controls labelled” was measured with the price list families collapsed; the real figure once expanded was 611 of 611. Expand every family, select all, add a custom line and enable volume tiers first.

Verification

A restored button label is not a pass

The .catch() restores the caption too, so a failure reads as a pass. Assert that a download fired and no alert appeared.

Verification

Compare numbers, not formatted strings

68.73% and 68.7% are the same number at two precisions. Comparing the strings produced a false “the tabs disagree” failure.

Verification

A half-applied edit passes unit-level checks

A script that asserted and aborted left a consumer without its declarations (mkAfter is not defined). Only driving the real file input caught it. Test end to end.

Tooling

grep -c counts lines, not matches

This file has single lines megabytes long — one of them is over a megabyte on its own. Use grep -o … | wc -l.

Tooling

Heredocs mangle edit scripts here

Write the script to a file, then run it.

Tooling

Verify every deploy copy by md5

Three copies live outside git and are updated by hand after every merge. Do not assume the copy ran.

Tooling · contract

buildExportObj() is an untyped contract

Its output shape ties the web app to generate_pdf.py, with nothing enforcing it. Change what goes into a line item and both the pdfmake builder and the Python script need matching updates, or the PDFs lose data or throw.

H

Open items

Three carried forward, in the order they are worth attacking.

H.1 · Data, not code

Manufacturing cost exists for 1 of 86 products

Margin is built, tested and mirrors the workbook exactly — and has nothing to work on. The workbook itself says cost must come from Monitor. Until then it is typed in per product in the picker.

Two questions the meeting spec asks the business to answer: how current the cost will be, and who owns the refresh. A silently stale cost is worse than no cost.

Watch for If cost lands in the master price list, that workbook becomes an internal document and must not be forwarded to customers as-is.

H.2 · Code

The price list archive is write-only

plSaveToArchive() writes cc_pricelist_archive_v1, mints an ID and confirms with a toast. Nothing ever reads it back — no list, no loader, no UI. Every saved price list is unreachable, and the Archive drawer in the top bar is the quote archive only.

Either Build a loader mirroring renderArchiveList / openQuote — noting the saved record also omits basisColumn, which must be added first or a restored list would price from the wrong column. Or Remove the Save button, so it stops promising something it does not do.

H.3 · Source data

One part number, two prices, in the 2024 workbook

C000082-26 appears twice in the Cables section as two different products: row 159, “Straight M12 to RJ45 male, 2m”, €61.00; and row 180, “Ethernet cable adapter V700”, €45.00. Verified by reading the xlsx directly — not a parser bug. It also shows up live: “Select all” reports 128 selected, not 129, because selection is keyed by part number and the two collapse into one.

Any price list including Cables shows the same part number twice at two prices, and because selection is keyed by part number the two cannot be picked apart. The app warns; the fix belongs in the workbook.

Before Sending any price list that includes Cables.

H.4 · Deferred by choice

Tier B/C UI, and the declined redesign items

Left undone deliberately: the archive drawer row treatment, responsive behaviour below 1280px, and the empty band between panel and document at wide widths.

The redesign brief's product-card grid and configurator grouping were declined — they assume a CPQ model this tool does not have. Variants are baked into the SKU, so cards would be worse than search.
