# v1.9.0 — Quote document restyle and whole-unit prices

**Approved 2026-09-17 against the mockup**
https://claude.ai/artifact/5u1rWtVmQocJN3C23s4hds (version 3). Every item below was
seen on the mockup before any code was written; do not reverse silently.

Scope: the **quote** document only, on all three surfaces (screen `#docRoot`, the pdfmake
PDF, `generate_pdf.py`). The price list document keeps Poppins/Roboto and its orange rule.

## 1. Header

- **The 4 pt orange rule under the header is removed** on every page. The orange stays in
  the quote number, the TO label, the section eyebrows and the total.
- **Logo 150 pt → 200 pt wide** in the pdfmake header. Screen and WeasyPrint scale by the
  same 4/3: 56 px → 75 px and 64 px → 85 px tall.
- **+30 pt between the header and PRICE QUOTE.** pdfmake `pageMargins` top 92 → 122. The
  200 pt logo is 16 pt taller than the 150 pt one, so without this it sits on the title.

## 2. Font — Arial throughout the quote

- Screen: every `'Poppins'` in a quote-document selector becomes Arial; weights 800 → 700.
- pdfmake: **Helvetica, pdfmake's built-in standard font, not an embedded Arial.** Owner's
  call 2026-09-17: nothing new is embedded, no licensing question, and Windows / Adobe
  Reader substitute Arial for a non-embedded Helvetica, which is metrically identical.
  `defaultStyle.font` and every `HEAD_FONT` use become Helvetica. Poppins stays registered
  because the price list builder still uses it.
- `generate_pdf.py`: every `'Poppins'` in the CSS becomes Arial. The two `@font-face`
  blocks and the `poppins-*-b64.txt` reads are then unused and are removed. **The script
  no longer needs the Poppins files beside it** — HANDOFF §7 delivery notes change.

## 3. Marketing's two requests

- **Part number more visible.** `.psku`: 9 pt grey → 10 pt **bold**, same ink as the
  product name. All three surfaces.
- **UNIT PRICE / QTY headers too close.** The columns are `auto` width with 6 pt padding, so
  the UNIT PRICE column is only as wide as the price and its heading wraps to two lines,
  the second landing against QTY. Fix: the heading no longer wraps (`noWrap` /
  `white-space:nowrap`), and the Qty column gets 22 pt padding on both sides instead of 6.

## 4. "Round prices to whole units" checkbox

- A checkbox in the **Terms card of the left panel**, under "Show list price column" and
  "State the 12-month forecast condition". Label: `Round prices to whole units
  (€1,256.72 → €1,257)`. **Off by default**, for a new quote and for every archived quote
  written before it existed.
- When on: **the unit price is rounded to a whole unit of the display currency, and the
  line total is that rounded unit × qty.** €658.26 × 2 prints €658 × 2 = €1,316 — not
  €1,317, which is what rounding the €1,316.52 total on its own gives. The subtotal is the
  sum of the rounded line totals. This is the same rule as `lineTotalFromDisplayedUnit`
  (HANDOFF §27, §29): the document's own figures must multiply out.
- Money on the quote prints with 0 decimals when on, 2 when off. Applies to unit price,
  list price, line total, subtotal and total. Custom volume-tier matrix prices are typed
  verbatim and are not touched.
- Carried as `round_prices` in `buildExportObj()` (so both PDF paths honour it) and in the
  archive snapshot (so it survives reopen). Renderers treat a missing key as off.
- Price list tab: unaffected. It has its own `plFmtMoney`.
