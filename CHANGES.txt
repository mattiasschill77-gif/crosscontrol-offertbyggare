CC QUOTE BUILDER — WHAT'S CHANGED
=================================
Current build: v1.9.1 (12 October 2026)

Open the folder named for the version, and double-click the .html file inside it.
The version is shown in the top bar of the tool, so you can always tell which
build you have open.


v1.9.1 — 12 October 2026
------------------------
FIXED: a custom item's price was treated as euros, whatever currency you had
chosen.

If you added a custom item (for example bespoke engineering work) while the quote
was set to USD or SEK, the price you typed was converted again on its way to the
document:

    typed 9500 with SEK chosen   ->  the quote said 108 300 kr
    typed 9500 with USD chosen   ->  the quote said $10 260

The price now stays exactly what you type, in the currency you have chosen, and
the box shows the currency symbol so you can see which one you are typing in.
Switching currency afterwards converts the price instead of re-reading it.

Quotes in EUR were never affected.


v1.9.0 — 17 September 2026
--------------------------
NEW: a cleaner quote document. Larger logo, more room above the heading, no rule
under the header, Arial throughout, and the part number is easier to read. The
price list document is unchanged.

NEW: "Round prices to whole units" — a checkbox under Terms. Off by default. With
it on, prices print without decimals, and the line total is the rounded unit price
times the quantity, so the figures on the page still multiply out.


v1.8.3 — 9 September 2026
-------------------------
FIXED: prices in the PDF could be a fraction off in USD and SEK.

The PDF rounded amounts before converting them to your chosen currency, so a
small difference came back multiplied by the exchange rate. The screen was right
and the PDF was not — so the two disagreed. Worst case seen: a price typed as
2 750,00 kr printed as 2 750,02 kr.

FIXED: a unit price typed over a volume tier did not reach the document or get
saved. The preview kept showing the tier price until you touched another field,
and if you closed the tab the typed price was lost.

Quotes in EUR were never affected by the rounding.


v1.8.2 — 8 September 2026
-------------------------
FIXED: the Unit price box sat on top of the volume-tier buttons.


v1.8.1 — 8 September 2026
-------------------------
FIXED: special characters in a customer name, product description or note could
come out wrong in the PDF produced by the separate Python script. A name such as
"Wilson & Sons" now prints correctly.


v1.8.0 — 8 September 2026
-------------------------
NEW: the customer's address on the quote, under the company name — and on the
price list, which now uses the same addressee block.

NEW: "Show list price column" — a checkbox under Terms, on by default. Turn it off
and the customer sees the unit price only, with no list price and no discount note.

NEW: type a unit price directly over a volume tier. The typed price wins, the
extra discount is cleared while it is set, and a warning shows whenever the price
differs from the tier price — above it as well as below. "Use tier price" puts it
back. Clicking a different tier keeps the price you typed.

FIXED, price list PDF: a family heading could print at the foot of a page with its
products starting on the next one.

FIXED, price list PDF: the column headings (PRODUCT / NET PRICE / MOQ) appeared
above the first product family only.

FIXED, price list PDF: prices could break across two lines — "2 438,6" on one line
and "0" on the next. This affected most four-figure prices.

FIXED, quote: the unit price times the quantity did not equal the printed total in
USD or SEK. A quote showing 1 042,29 x 15 printed a total of 15 634,30 when the
multiplication gives 15 634,35.


IF YOU HAVE SENT QUOTES FROM AN OLDER BUILD
-------------------------------------------
Several of the fixes above changed printed figures, and all of them only affected
quotes in USD or SEK — a quote written in EUR was correct in every build.

Worth a look if you sent non-EUR quotes from a build older than this one:

  - custom items priced before v1.9.1 (the price was converted twice)
  - totals and prices before v1.8.3 (small rounding differences)
  - line totals before v1.8.0 (unit price x quantity did not match the total)

The archive keeps every quote, so you can reopen one and compare.
